| **Generic/Other** | ~30 | ~100 | 3% | ❌ Poor |

### Content Pattern Validation

**What Works (VALIDATED):**
1. **Activity Listicles** - "Topp 5 aktiviteter barn Oslo" pattern
   - High engagement (78%+)
   - Good CTR when well-positioned
   - Best format for Norway

2. **Specific Road Trips** - "2 dagers tur Hardangervidda"
   - Exceptional CTR (12.99%)
   - Low competition
   - High rental intent

3. **Wedding Content** - "Lei bil til bryllupet"
   - Top performer (788 clicks)
   - Clear rental intent
   - High conversion potential

4. **Owner Guides** - "Disse bilene bør du leie ut"
   - Strong engagement (90%)
   - Supply-side content works

**What Doesn't Work:**
1. **Generic Price Content** - "bilutleie-pris", "hva-koster-leiebil"
   - High impressions, terrible CTR (<0.5%)
   - Competing with aggregators

2. **Maintenance Tips** - "vinterdekk-tips"
   - Vanity traffic - users already have cars
   - Not rental-relevant
