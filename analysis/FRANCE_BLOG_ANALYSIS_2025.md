## Content Pattern Validation

### What Works in France (VALIDATED)

1. **B2B/Professional Guides** ✅
   - Professional vehicle displacement (10.9K clicks)
   - Company car regulations (8.4K clicks)
   - Tax implications (TVS, TVA)
   - **Format:** Detailed explainers with calculations

2. **ZFE Regulatory Content** ✅
   - City-specific guides (Toulouse, Reims, Grenoble)
   - Exemption lists
   - **Format:** Year-stamped, regularly updated

3. **Van Aménagé Content** ✅
   - Spot guides (where to park/sleep)
   - European road trip itineraries
   - Family van travel
   - **Format:** Practical guides with locations

4. **Owner Profitability Content** ✅
   - "How much can I earn" calculators
   - Best cars to rent out
   - **Format:** Numbers-driven, practical advice

### What Underperforms

1. **Generic City Guides** ⚠️
   - "Visites autour de [city]" - high impressions, low CTR
   - Too broad, not specific to rental use case

2. **Olympics 2024 Content** ❌
   - Now outdated, generating few clicks
   - Should be updated or removed

3. **Pagination Pages** ❌
   - /blog/page/2/, etc. - diluting authority
