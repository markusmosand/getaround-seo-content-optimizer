## Key Findings Summary

### Major 2026 Trends

1. **GEO/AEO is now essential** - Equal priority to traditional SEO
2. **60% of searches end without clicks** - AI Overviews consuming traffic
3. **Search is fragmenting** - Users across Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Reddit
4. **Human expertise differentiating** - AI content being devalued
5. **Answer-first content wins** - Structure for AI parsing critical

### Gartner Prediction
25% of organic search traffic will shift to AI chatbots by 2026.

### Google's Position
"Good SEO is good GEO" - but acknowledges search traffic decline is inevitable.

## Implications for Getaround

### Content Structure Changes
| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| Keyword-focused | Answer-focused |
| Long introductions | Direct answers first |
| SEO titles | Question-style headings |
| Text-only | Multi-format (video, FAQ) |

### Schema Markup Priority
1. FAQPage - Critical for Q&A content
2. HowTo - For step-by-step guides
3. Article - With author info for E-E-A-T
4. LocalBusiness - For location pages

### New KPIs to Track
- Featured snippet presence
- AI Overview appearance
- Brand mentions in ChatGPT/Perplexity
- Zero-click traffic analysis

### France-Specific
- FAQPage schema on ZFE content
- Expert author bios on B2B content
- Year-stamping for regulatory freshness

### Norway-Specific
- HowTo schema on road trip guides
- Local experience signals
- Question-style headings for activities
