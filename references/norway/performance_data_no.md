# Blog Performance Insights - Norway Market

Data-driven insights from Getaround Norway blog content.

**Data Period:** Last 90 days (August-November 2025)
**Source:** Google Analytics, Google Search Console
**Last Updated:** November 2025

---

## Executive Summary

Norwegian blog performance is dominated by **consumer activity content** - listicles about things to do in cities perform exceptionally well, particularly for families.

### Key Findings

**Traffic Composition:**
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family activity content: Top performers
- 🏔️ Outdoor/nature content: Strong engagement
- 👤 Owner guides: High engagement, lower volume
- 📍 Oslo content: Majority of traffic

**Critical Insight:** Unlike France where B2B content dominates, Norway's success comes from consumer-focused activity listicles that inspire travel and create car rental need.

## What Works in Norway (Proven Patterns)

### ✅ Format 1: Activity Listicles

**Pattern:** "Topp [5-10] [aktiviteter/ting å gjøre] i [By]"

**Characteristics:**
- Length: 1,200-1,800 words
- Tone: Warm, friendly "du"
- Structure: Intro → Numbered list → Practical section → CTA

**Must include:**
- 5-10 numbered items
- 150-200 words per activity
- Practical info: parking, pricing, best time
- Family-friendly indicators where relevant
- Natural Getaround integration (not pushy)
- FAQ at end (3-5 questions)

**Engagement targets:**
- Engagement rate: 65-80%
- Time on page: 25-40 seconds

**Examples that work:**
- "Topp 5 aktiviteter for barn i Oslo"
- "Topp 10 ting å gjøre i Tromsø"
- "De beste vandreturene rundt Bergen"

### ✅ Format 2: Owner Guides (How-to)

**Pattern:** "[Action verb] + [konkret tema]"

**Characteristics:**
- Length: 1,800-2,500 words
- Tone: Friendly but concrete "du"
- Structure: Problem → Solution → Step-by-step → Data

**Must include:**
- Concrete income examples
- ROI calculations or examples
- Step-by-step processes
- Address common concerns
- FAQ format works extremely well

**Engagement targets:**
- Engagement rate: 75-90%
- Time on page: 45-85 seconds

**Examples that work:**
- "Disse bilene bør du leie ut"
- "Slik optimaliserer du inntektene dine"
- "Alt du trenger å vite om utleie"

### ✅ Format 3: Use-Case Articles

**Pattern:** "[Lei/Leie] [specific type] til [specific occasion]"

**Characteristics:**
- Length: 800-1,200 words
- Tone: Helpful, solution-focused
- Structure: Problem → Solution → How-to → CTA

**Engagement targets:**
- Engagement rate: 80-90%
- Time on page: 15-25 seconds (shorter is OK if they convert)

**Examples that work:**
- "Lei en bil til bryllupet ditt"
- "Leie varebil til flytting"
- "Lei bil til eksamen (kjøreprøve)"

## What Doesn't Work in Norway (Avoid These)

### ❌ Case Studies Without Data

| Article | Sessions | Engagement |
|---------|----------|------------|
| Emelie og Lasse bygde campervan | 181 | 49% |
| Aaron og Lena leier ut bil | 132 | 45% |
| Møt Sandra... | 32 | 47% |

**Why it fails:**
- Wrong search intent: Users search "how to rent out car", not "read about Sandra"
- Missing credibility markers: No data, numbers, or concrete takeaways
- Weak structure: Narrative form without scannable elements
- Wrong funnel placement – should be social media content, not SEO

**How to fix:**
- Add concrete data (monthly income, utilization rate, ROI)
- Structure with H2/H3 subheadings
- Include actionable takeaways
- Lead with benefits, then tell story

### ❌ Content-Product Mismatch

**Example: "Skifte til vinterdekk"**
- 255 sessions, but only 47% engagement
- 4,570 impressions, 5 clicks (0.11% CTR)

**Problem:**
- Topic is seasonal (October-November)
- Low match with Getaround's core product
- Users bounce because content doesn't meet their need

**This is "vanity traffic"** - high impressions, but ZERO business value.

**Fix:**
- Pivot to "Leie bil med vinterdekk – hva du må vite" (product relevance)
- OR deindex/redirect to more relevant content

### ❌ Geographic Mismatch (Low Volume)

Bergen, Stavanger, Trondheim: Good engagement but very low traffic vs Oslo.

| Article | Sessions | Engagement |
|---------|----------|------------|
| Topp 5 aktiviteter barn Trondheim | 132 | 89% |
| Topp 5 vandreturer Stavanger | 226 | 61% |

**These perform well by quality, but volume is too low to prioritize.**

**Strategy:**
- Prioritize Oslo content (where the volume is)
- Create strategic "city collections" for breadth
- Consider "things to do in Norway" umbrella content
