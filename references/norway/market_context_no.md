# Getaround Norway - Market Context

Essential information about Getaround Norway for content creation.

---

## What Getaround Is

Getaround is a peer-to-peer car-sharing marketplace. Private owners and small professional actors list their cars, and renters book via app or web. Selection ranges from small cars and vans to EVs and RVs.

---

## Products & Delivery Methods

- **Getaround Connect (hardware):** Unlock and start car with phone. Requires installed box. No physical handover.
- **Native Connect:** Via Smartcar API for Tesla and other compatible vehicles, no hardware needed. Only available in Norway.
- **Key handover:** Owner and renter meet for key exchange.

**Important:** Each car has ONE method enabled - either Connect (including Native Connect for Tesla) OR key handover. No switching per car or per rental.

### Product Details

| Element | Detaljer |

### Norway-Exclusive Features
- **Native Connect (Smartcar):** Tesla uten hardware-installasjon
- **Langtidsleie:** 30–180 dager med 2 000 km/mnd
- **Bobil (motorhome):** Tilgjengelig for utleie

- **Long-term rental:** 30-180 days (unique to Norway). 2,000 km included per month.
- **Getaround for Business (B2B):** For SMBs. Use from projects to ongoing needs. Vans constitute a large share of trips.

## Competitors & Threats

### Norway Market

| Competitor | Type | Threat Level |
|------------|------|--------------|
| Hyre | Direct competitor | High (main threat) |
| Bilkollektivet | Car cooperative | Medium |
| Traditional rental | Europcar, Hertz, etc. | Medium |

**Competitive dynamics:**
- **Renter side:** Hyre is the clearest threat. Traditional players can pressure price.
- **Owner side:** Practically a monopoly. No other scaled platforms for listing own car, except niches (RV sites) and FINN.no at smaller scale.
- **Organic search:** Hyre and traditional players often rank strongly.

## Rules, Insurance & Qualifications

- **Age & license:** Minimum requirements apply
- **Insurance:** Included, with three levels that reduce deductible for extra daily cost
- **Fuel:** Renter pays difference if deviation from level at pickup

## Owner Side

### Motivation
- Side income and scaling
- Lower barrier than property rental
- Income varies by car type and availability

### Common Concerns
- Damage and wear
- Insurance coverage
- Cleaning and maintenance

### Measures
- Photo documentation at check-in/out
- Clear responsibility guidelines
- Insurance during all rentals

## Challenges & Improvement Areas

### Oslo Market
- Hyre is aggressive on price
- Hyre has newer car fleet

### Quality Consistency
- Cleaning levels vary (cars are privately owned)
- Hyre has fixed washing programs

### Service
- Customer service and damage handling improving
- Response time and quality focus ongoing

- Avoid exaggerations
- Explain internal terms briefly
- Paint pictures of experiences
