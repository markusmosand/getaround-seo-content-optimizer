# Getaround France - Market Context

Essential information about Getaround France for content creation.

---

## What Getaround Is

Getaround is a peer-to-peer car-sharing marketplace. Private owners (propriétaires) and professional operators list their cars, and renters (locataires) book via app or web. Selection ranges from city cars (citadines) and vans (utilitaires) to electric vehicles and premium cars.

**French market position:** Leading peer-to-peer car-sharing platform in France, following the acquisition of Drivy.

---

## Products & Delivery Methods

### Rental Types

- **Getaround Connect (hardware):** Unlock and start car with phone. Requires installed box. No physical handover. Most convenient for both parties.
- **Key handover (Remise en main propre):** Owner and renter meet for key exchange. More personal but requires coordination.

**Important:** Each car has ONE method enabled - either Connect OR key handover. No switching per car or per rental.

### Product Details

| Élément | Détails |
|---------|---------|
| Abonnement Connect | 29 €/mois (couvre la connectivité) |
| Partenaire assurance | AXA (confirmé) |
| Valeur max véhicule | 50 000 € |

### Features NOT Available in France

| Feature | Availability |
|---------|--------------|
| Native Connect (Smartcar) | Norway only |
| Long-term rental (30-180 days) | Norway only |
| Motorhome (Bobil) | Norway only |

Note: Campervan and utilitaires (vans) ARE available in France.

### Additional Services

- **Delivery (Livraison):** Owner can deliver car to renter's chosen address. Requires 24h advance booking and 48h minimum rental.
- **Getaround for Business (B2B):** For companies. Fleet access for business needs. Vans and professional vehicles popular. Requires SIRET number. 2,000+ companies using the service, presence in 500+ train stations.

## French Market Specifics

### Competitors & Threats

| Competitor | Type | Threat Level | Strengths |
|------------|------|--------------|-----------|
| Turo | P2P car-sharing | High | Growing brand, similar model |
| Virtuo | App-based rental | Medium | Premium focus, modern UX |
| Europcar/Hertz/Avis | Traditional | Medium | Airport dominance, brand trust |
| Ouicar | P2P | Low | Smaller scale |
| Sixt | Traditional | Medium | Competitive pricing |

**Competitive positioning:**
- Getaround strength: Neighborhood availability, peer-to-peer pricing, flexibility
- Getaround weakness: Less airport presence than traditional players
- Opportunity: B2B/professional segment underserved by competitors

### Regulatory Environment (CRITICAL for France)

**ZFE (Zones à Faibles Émissions):**
- Paris: Active since 2019, expanding annually
- Lyon: Implementing 2025
- Marseille: Active
- More cities following (Bordeaux, Toulouse, Nice)

**Impact on content:** ZFE compliance is a major search driver and rental decision factor.

**Crit'Air Vignette:**
- Required classification for all vehicles
- Determines ZFE access rights
- Categories 0-5 based on emissions

**Professional Vehicle Regulations:**
- Indemnités kilométriques (mileage reimbursement)
- TVS (Taxe sur les Véhicules de Sociétés)
- Avantage en nature véhicule de fonction
- Barème fiscal updated annually

- Weekend getaways from major cities
- Seasonal activities (ski, beach, wine harvest)
- Airport and logistics guides

**Tone:** L'Adventure Lab style - inspiring, warm "vous", practical poetry

### B2B/Professional (60% of content)

**Topics:**
- Professional vehicle usage guides
- Fiscal optimization (IK, TVS, avantage en nature)
- ZFE compliance guides by city
- Fleet management alternatives
- TCO comparisons (rental vs. purchase vs. leasing)

**Tone:** Professional, authoritative, data-driven, formal "vous"
