# Blog Performance Insights - France Market

Data-driven insights for Getaround France blog content optimization.

**Data Period:** Last 90 days (August-November 2025)
**Source:** Google Search Console (fr.getaround.com)
**Last Updated:** November 2025

---

## Executive Summary

The French Getaround blog demonstrates a dramatically different performance pattern from Norway, with **B2B and professional content dominating** traffic and engagement.

### Key Findings

**Traffic Composition:**
- 🏢 B2B/Professional content: **80% of top 5 blog traffic** (~25,000+ clicks)
- 👨‍💼 Top performer: "Déplacement professionnel véhicule personnel" - **9,737 clicks/month**
- 📋 Regulatory content: Highest impression volume (ZFE: **174,554 impressions**)
- 👥 Consumer/leisure content: Underrepresented compared to market potential

**Critical Insight:** Unlike Norway where consumer activity content dominates, France's blog success comes from professional vehicle usage and regulatory compliance content.

## What Works in France (Proven Patterns)

### ✅ Format 1: Professional Guides (B2B)

**Pattern:** "Guide complet : [Professional topic] en [Year]"

**Characteristics:**
- Length: 1,800-2,500 words
- Tone: Formal "vous", professional, authoritative
- Structure: Problem → Regulation → Solution → Calculation/Tool

**Must include:**
- Current year in title/H1 (2024, 2025)
- Regulatory references (Code du travail, URSSAF, etc.)
- Practical examples with numbers
- Comparison tables
- FAQ section (10-15 questions)

**Engagement targets:**
- Engagement rate: 70-85%
- Time on page: 45-65 seconds
- Conversion focus: Professional user sign-up or B2B inquiry

**Examples that work:**
- "Véhicule de fonction vs véhicule de service : Guide 2025"
- "Location longue durée professionnelle : calcul du coût réel"
- "Déplacement professionnel : optimiser vos frais en 2025"

### ✅ Format 2: Regulatory Explainers

**Pattern:** "Tout savoir sur [Regulation] en [Year]"

**Characteristics:**
- Length: 2,000-3,000 words
- Tone: Educational but authoritative
- Structure: What → Why → When → How → Where

**Must include:**
- Timeline of changes (2024 → 2025 → 2026)
- Geographic specificity (which cities, when)
- Visual aids (maps, tables, infographics)
- Practical action steps
- Impact on rental decisions

**Engagement targets:**
- Engagement rate: 65-80%
- Time on page: 50-70 seconds
- Conversion focus: Direct booking (need compliant vehicle)

**Examples that work:**
- "ZFE Paris 2025 : calendrier, véhicules autorisés, sanctions"
- "TVS 2025 : calcul et optimisation pour flottes professionnelles"
- "Crit'Air : obtention et zones concernées en France"

### ✅ Format 3: TCO Calculators & Comparisons

**Pattern:** "[Option A] vs [Option B] : coût réel pour professionnels"

**Characteristics:**
- Length: 1,500-2,000 words
- Tone: Analytical, data-driven
- Structure: Scenario → Costs → Comparison → Recommendation

**Must include:**
- Interactive calculator (or detailed formula)
- Real numbers and examples
- TCO breakdown (acquisition, maintenance, fuel, tax, insurance)
- Decision framework

**Engagement targets:**
- Engagement rate: 75-85%
- Time on page: 55-75 seconds
- Conversion focus: Long-term rental or B2B contract

**Examples that work:**
- "Location vs achat professionnel : analyse sur 3 ans"
- "Flotte en leasing vs Getaround : étude de cas PME"
- "Véhicule électrique professionnel : rentabilité réelle 2025"

### ✅ Format 4: Consumer Inspiration (L'Adventure Lab Style)

**Pattern:** Narrative-driven adventure content

**Characteristics:**
- Length: 1,500-2,000 words
- Tone: Warm "vous" with personality (L'Adventure Lab standard)
- Structure: Hook → Journey → Practical → Invitation

**Must include:**
- Sensory, immersive opening
- Authentic French voice
- Practical details woven into narrative
- Soft CTA (invitation, not push)

**Engagement targets:**
- Engagement rate: 65-80%
- Time on page: 40-55 seconds
- Conversion focus: Indirect (inspiration → booking)

## What Doesn't Work in France (Avoid These)

### ❌ Generic Tourist Content (Without Professional Angle)

**Problem:** Generic "things to do in Paris" without car rental relevance
- Similar to Norwegian "skifte til vinterdekk" problem
- High impressions, low conversion
- Not differentiated from travel agencies

**Exception:** International road trips DO work (France → Spain proven success)

### ❌ Consumer Case Studies Without Data

**Problem:** "Meet Pierre who rents out his Renault Clio"
- Low engagement in French market (professional skepticism)
- Need concrete numbers: "Pierre earns €487/month with 67% utilization"
- Without data = perceived as marketing fluff

### ❌ Surface-Level Regulatory Content

**Problem:** "ZFE zones exist in France" without depth
- French searches are sophisticated and specific
- Need year-specific timelines, city-by-city details, exemptions
- Shallow content gets outranked by government sites

### ❌ Translated English Content

**Problem:** Content that feels translated from English
- French readers detect inauthenticity immediately
- Must be written natively in French
- Use French expressions, not English-to-French translations

## Geographic Priorities

### Tier 1: Major Metropolitan Areas (Primary Focus)

**Paris & Île-de-France**
- Largest market (12M people + 30M tourists)
- ZFE zone = regulatory content opportunity
- Both B2B and consumer demand
- Airport focus: CDG, Orly

**Lyon**
- 2nd largest metro (2.3M)
- ZFE zone starting 2025
- Strong B2B market (business hub)
- Airport: Lyon-Saint-Exupéry

**Marseille**
- 3rd largest metro (1.9M)
- Gateway to Provence/Côte d'Azur
- Tourist + professional mix
- Airport: Marseille-Provence

### Tier 2: Secondary Cities

- **Bordeaux** - Wine tourism + business
- **Toulouse** - Aerospace hub (B2B potential)
- **Nice** - Tourist gateway + Côte d'Azur
- **Nantes** - Growing business center
- **Strasbourg** - EU institutions, German border
