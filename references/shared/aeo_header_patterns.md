# AEO Header Patterns for Getaround Content

Guidelines for structuring headers and content to rank in AI Overviews, Featured Snippets, and traditional search.

---

## Part 1: Why Header Structure Matters for AEO

### The Shift to Answer Engines (2025-2026)

**Key Statistics:**
- AI Overviews trigger on 13%+ of queries (up from 6.5% in early 2025)
- 34.5% YoY drop in position-1 CTR for queries with AI Overviews
- Gartner predicts 25% traditional search volume drop by 2026
- AI systems cite content with clear Q&A structure more often

**What This Means for Getaround:**
- Content must be structured for BOTH traditional SEO AND AI extraction
- Headers should match actual search queries
- Answers must be immediately extractable (no buried information)
- Clear hierarchy helps AI understand content relationships

### How AI Systems Parse Content

AI models (Google AI Overview, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude) look for:

1. **Clear question signals** - Headers phrased as questions
2. **Immediate answers** - Direct response in first 40-60 words after header
3. **Structured formats** - Lists, tables, defined patterns
4. **Authority signals** - Data, citations, expertise markers
5. **Semantic completeness** - Each section fully answers its question

## Part 2: Question-Format Headers

### Why Question Headers Win

Question-format headers directly match how users search:
- Voice searches are naturally questions
- "People Also Ask" queries are questions
- AI prompts are often questions
- Questions create clear expectations for answers

### Question Header Patterns

**Definition Questions (What/Who)**
```
## Hva er bildeling?
## Qu'est-ce que l'autopartage ?
## What is car-sharing?

Best for: Introductory content, glossary sections, concept explanations
Snippet type: Paragraph (40-60 words)
```

**Process Questions (How)**
```
## Hvordan booker jeg en bil på Getaround?
## Comment réserver une voiture sur Getaround ?
## How do I book a car on Getaround?

Best for: How-to guides, tutorials, step-by-step content
Snippet type: Ordered list or paragraph
```

**Cost Questions (How much)**
```
## Hvor mye koster det å leie bil i Oslo?
## Combien coûte la location d'une voiture à Paris ?
## How much does it cost to rent a car in Oslo?

Best for: Pricing pages, comparison content, budget guides
Snippet type: Paragraph + table
```

**Location Questions (Where)**
```
## Hvor finner jeg biler i nærheten?
## Où trouver des voitures à proximité ?
## Where can I find cars nearby?

Best for: City guides, location content, availability info
Snippet type: Paragraph or list
```

**Comparison Questions (Which/What's better)**
```
## Hva er forskjellen mellom Getaround og tradisjonell bilutleie?
## Quelle est la différence entre Getaround et la location traditionnelle ?
## Which is better: Getaround or traditional car rental?

Best for: Comparison articles, decision-support content
Snippet type: Table or paragraph
```

**Requirement Questions (Do I need/Can I)**
```
## Trenger jeg forsikring når jeg leier bil?
## Ai-je besoin d'une assurance pour louer une voiture ?
## Do I need insurance when renting a car?

Best for: FAQ sections, requirement clarifications
Snippet type: Paragraph
```

## Part 3: Answer-First Paragraph Structure

### The 40-60 Word Answer Block

After every question-format header, immediately provide a direct, self-contained answer:

**Structure:**
```
[Question Header]

[Direct answer: 40-60 words that fully answer the question]

[Supporting details, examples, elaboration...]
```

**Why 40-60 Words?**
- Google's featured snippets average 40-60 words
- AI systems extract complete thoughts
- Short enough to be quote-ready
- Long enough to be comprehensive

### Answer Block Formula

**Formula:** Direct answer + Key qualifier + Specific example/data

**Template:**
```
[Topic] [action/state] [answer]. [Key qualifier or context]. [Specific example with number/data if applicable].
```

### Examples by Question Type

**Definition Answer:**
```
## Hva er bildeling?

Bildeling er en tjeneste der privatpersoner leier ut bilene sine til andre via en plattform som Getaround. I motsetning til tradisjonell bilutleie, hvor selskaper eier bilflåten, er bildeling peer-to-peer: du leier direkte fra en privatperson, ofte til lavere priser og med større fleksibilitet.

[Utdypende forklaring følger...]
```

**Process Answer:**
```
## Hvordan booker jeg en bil på Getaround?

For å booke en bil på Getaround, søker du etter biler i nærheten via appen eller nettsiden, velger ønsket bil og tidsrom, og bekrefter bookingen med betaling. Hele prosessen tar under 2 minutter, og du får tilgang til bilen direkte via telefonen med Connect-teknologi.

[Steg-for-steg guide følger...]
```

**Cost Answer:**
```
## Hvor mye koster det å leie bil i Oslo?

Billeie i Oslo koster typisk 200-800 kr per dag, avhengig av biltype og sesong. En kompakt elbil starter på rundt 200-350 kr/dag, mens premium-biler som Tesla Model 3 ligger på 500-800 kr/dag. Langtidsleie over 7 dager gir ofte 20-30% rabatt.

[Pristabell og detaljer følger...]
```

**Comparison Answer:**
```
## Hva er forskjellen mellom Getaround og tradisjonell bilutleie?

Hovedforskjellen er at Getaround er peer-to-peer bildeling der du leier fra privatpersoner, mens tradisjonell bilutleie er fra selskaper med egen bilflåte. Getaround er ofte 30-40% billigere, mer fleksibel (time til 90 dager), og biler er tilgjengelig i flere nabolag.

[Sammenligning følger...]
```

### Answer Block Checklist

- [ ] Starts immediately after header (no filler intro)
- [ ] 40-60 words (count!)
- [ ] Self-contained (makes sense without context)
- [ ] Directly answers the question
- [ ] Includes specific data point if relevant
- [ ] Declarative tone (statements, not questions)
- [ ] No "In this section we will..." type intros

## Part 4: Featured Snippet Optimization

### Snippet Types and When to Use Them

**Paragraph Snippets (40-60 words)**
Best for:
- Definition queries ("what is...")
- Simple explanations
- Single-answer questions

Structure:
```
## [Question]

[Direct 40-60 word answer that fully addresses the question.]
```

**List Snippets (Ordered)**
Best for:
- Step-by-step processes
- Ranked items
- Sequential instructions

Structure:
```
## Hvordan [prosess]?

For å [oppnå resultat], følg disse stegene:

1. [Første steg]
2. [Andre steg]
3. [Tredje steg]
4. [Fjerde steg]
```

**List Snippets (Unordered)**
Best for:
- Features or benefits
- Requirements
- Options without ranking

Structure:
```
## Hva er fordelene med [emne]?

Hovedfordelene med [emne] inkluderer:

- [Fordel 1]
- [Fordel 2]
- [Fordel 3]
- [Fordel 4]
```

**Table Snippets**
Best for:
- Comparisons
- Pricing
- Specifications
- Feature matrices

Structure:
```
## [Sammenligning spørsmål]?

| Faktor | Alternativ A | Alternativ B |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| Pris   | X kr         | Y kr         |
| [...]  | [...]        | [...]        |
```

### Snippet Optimization Rules

**Do:**
- Place the answer immediately after the header
- Match the content format to the query type
- Use clean, simple HTML/Markdown structure
- Include the target keyword naturally
- Keep lists to 5-8 items max

**Don't:**
- Bury answers in long paragraphs
- Mix list types (ordered/unordered) in same section
- Use complex nested structures
- Include promotional language in answer blocks
- Start with "In this article..." or similar

## Part 5: Semantic Chunking

### One Sub-Question Per Section

Each H2 section should:
1. Answer ONE specific question completely
2. Be self-contained (could stand alone)
3. Have the key information at the top
4. Support with details below

**Good Example:**
```
## Hvor mye kan jeg tjene på å leie ut bilen min?

Du kan tjene 8000-15000 kr per måned ved å leie ut bilen din på Getaround, avhengig av biltype, lokasjon og tilgjengelighet. En Tesla Model 3 i Oslo med høy tilgjengelighet kan generere opptil 15000 kr/mnd, mens en standard personbil ligger nærmere 8000 kr/mnd.

### Faktorer som påvirker inntekten

[Detaljert forklaring...]

### Inntektseksempler per biltype

[Tabell med eksempler...]
```

**Bad Example (multiple questions mixed):**
```
## Om å leie ut bilen din

Mange lurer på om de kan leie ut bilen sin. Det er faktisk ganske enkelt. Du kan tjene penger, og prosessen er enkel. Først må du vurdere om bilen din kvalifiserer, deretter må du tenke på prising...

[Rotete blanding av hva, hvordan, og hvorfor]
```

### Quote-Worthy Sentence Placement

Place the most important, extractable statement:
- In the first sentence after the header
- In the answer block (40-60 words)
- Before any qualifications or exceptions

**Quote-worthy positioning:**
```
## Er forsikring inkludert i Getaround?

**Ja, full forsikring er inkludert i alle Getaround-bookinger.** ← Quote-worthy
Standarddekningen inkluderer ansvarsforsikring og kasko med 8000 kr egenandel.
Du kan oppgradere til redusert egenandel (3000 kr eller 0 kr) mot et daglig tillegg.
```

### The PAA/Featured Snippet Alignment Pattern

For maximum Featured Snippet and People Also Ask capture, align ALL key elements:

| Element | Value | Example |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Title Tag | The exact question | "Hvor mye koster det å leie bil i Oslo?" |
| URL Slug | The question (hyphenated) | `/hvor-mye-koster-leie-bil-oslo` |
| H1 | The exact question | Same as title |
| First Sentence | Direct answer | "Billeie i Oslo koster typisk 200-800 kr per dag." |

**Why this works:** Google's algorithm looks for consistency between these elements. When they all match the search query exactly, you signal maximum relevance.

**When to use:**
- Creating new content targeting PAA questions
- Optimizing content for specific long-tail queries identified in GSC
- Building "quick answer" pages for high-intent questions

## Part 6: Header Hierarchy Best Practices

### Proper Hierarchy Structure

```
# H1: Main Article Title (ONE per article)
   ## H2: Major Section 1
      ### H3: Subsection 1.1
      ### H3: Subsection 1.2
   ## H2: Major Section 2
      ### H3: Subsection 2.1
   ## H2: FAQ Section
      ### H3: Question 1
      ### H3: Question 2
```

### Hierarchy Rules

**Never Skip Levels:**
- ❌ H1 → H3 (skipped H2)
- ❌ H2 → H4 (skipped H3)
- ✅ H1 → H2 → H3

**H2 Guidelines:**
- 4-8 H2s per article (optimal)
- Each H2 should be scannable (user understands article from H2s alone)
- Include primary keyword in 1-2 H2s naturally
- Use question format for AEO optimization

**H3 Guidelines:**
- Use under H2 for subsections
- Don't overuse (not every paragraph needs H3)
- Keep parallel structure within same H2

### Scannable Header Test

A user should understand what the article covers by reading ONLY the headers:

**Good (tells the story):**
```
# Topp 5 aktiviteter for barn i Oslo
## Hvorfor Oslo er perfekt for familier
## 1. Vikingskipshuset
## 2. Tusenfryd
## 3. Oslo Sommerpark
## 4. Teknisk Museum
## 5. Holmenkollen
## Hvordan komme seg rundt i Oslo
## Ofte stilte spørsmål
```

**Bad (vague, unhelpful):**
```
# Aktiviteter i Oslo
## Introduksjon
## Attraksjon 1
## Attraksjon 2
## Mer info
## Oppsummering
```

## Part 9: H2 Content Injection Technique

### What is H2 Content Injection?

H2 Content Injection is a strategic technique where you add dedicated H2 sections targeting related high-volume queries within an existing article. Instead of creating separate articles for every query variation, you "inject" optimized answer sections into contextually relevant content.

### Why It Works

**Google rewards comprehensive content:**
- Articles that answer multiple related questions tend to rank higher
- PAA (People Also Ask) boxes often pull from content with multiple H2 Q&A sections
- Users stay longer when they find answers to follow-up questions

**Example Scenario:**
- Main article: "Best road trips from Oslo"
- Primary keyword: "road trips oslo"
- **Injected H2:** "How much does it cost to rent a car for a road trip?"
- **Result:** Captures traffic from the injected query while keeping main article relevance

### When to Use H2 Content Injection

**Good candidates for injection:**
- Related pricing questions ("hvor mye koster...")
- Requirement questions ("trenger jeg...", "må jeg ha...")
- Comparison questions ("hva er forskjellen mellom...")
- How-to questions that support the main topic

**Avoid injecting:**
- Off-topic questions that dilute the article focus
- Questions that deserve their own dedicated article
- Questions with completely different search intent

### H2 Injection Template

```markdown
## [Question targeting related high-volume query]?

[40-60 word direct answer following the answer-first format]

[1-2 supporting sentences with specific details, pricing, or data]

**Quick tip:** [One-sentence actionable advice related to the question]
```

### Getaround Examples

**Main article:** "Topp 5 aktiviteter for familier i Bergen"

**Injected H2:**
```markdown
## Hvor mye koster det å leie bil til familieturen?

Billeie for en familietur i Bergen koster typisk 300-500 kr per dag for en romslig stasjonsvogn eller SUV. Velger du en elbil, kan du spare på ferje- og bomavgifter, og prisene starter fra 250 kr/dag. Langtidsleie over 3 dager gir ofte 15-20% rabatt.

**Quick tip:** Book bilen minst 1 uke i forveien for bedre utvalg, spesielt i sommersesongen.
```

**Main article:** "Hvordan tjene penger på bilen din"

**Injected H2:**
```markdown
## Er det trygt å leie ut bilen min til fremmede?

Ja, bildeling via Getaround er trygt takket være flere sikkerhetslag. Alle leietakere er ID-verifisert og har gyldig førerkort. Full forsikring er inkludert i hver booking med dekning opptil 1 million kroner. I tillegg kan du se leietakers vurderinger fra tidligere bookinger før du godkjenner.

**Quick tip:** Start med å tillate bare leietakere med 4+ stjerner for ekstra trygghet.
```

### How to Identify Injection Opportunities

**From GSC data:**
1. Find PAA questions for your main keyword
2. Look for related queries in Search Console (filter by page)
3. Check Semrush/Ahrefs "Questions" report for related keywords

**From competitor analysis:**
1. See which H2s ranking articles use
2. Note FAQ questions on competing pages
3. Identify gaps your article doesn't cover

**From search behavior:**
1. Google's "People also ask" boxes
2. Related searches at bottom of SERPs
3. Forum questions (Reddit, Quora) about the topic

### Quality Control

**Before injecting an H2, verify:**
- [ ] The question is genuinely related to the main topic
- [ ] The injected section doesn't break article flow
- [ ] You have accurate, helpful information to provide
- [ ] The query has meaningful search volume (check GSC/Semrush)
- [ ] The answer follows the 40-60 word answer-first format

**Placement rules:**
- Place injected H2s in logical positions (not randomly mid-article)
- Group related injected questions together if adding multiple
- Don't add more than 2-3 injected H2s per article (maintain focus)
- Consider placing before the FAQ section for natural flow

- [ ] H1 includes primary keyword and is compelling

- [ ] At least 50% of H2s are question-format for AEO

- [ ] Proper hierarchy (no skipped levels)
- [ ] Headers alone tell the article's story (scannable)
- [ ] Answer blocks are self-contained and quote-ready
- [ ] Lists use clean structure (not buried in paragraphs)
- [ ] Tables are simple (limited columns/rows)
