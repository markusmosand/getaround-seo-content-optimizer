# Getaround Blog Performance Insights

Data-driven insights from 211 articles over 90 days (August-November 2025). Use these insights to inform content optimization decisions.

## Performance Benchmarks

**Overall metrics:**
- Total sessions: 14,160
- Median engagement rate: 67%
- Median engagement time: 24.9 seconds
- 75% of articles have <50 sessions (159 articles)
- Top 10 articles = 48% of all traffic
- Top 20 articles = 67% of all traffic

**Organic search (GSC):**
- 545 clicks (last 90 days) = ~182 clicks/month
- 98,373 impressions
- 0.55% CTR (industry standard: 2-3%)
- Pos 28.2 average
- 76% mobile (413 of 545 clicks)

## What Works Exceptionally Well

### 1. Listicles About Activities in Major Cities

**Top performers:**
- "Topp 5 aktiviteter for barn i Oslo": 1,293 sessions, 78% engagement rate
- "Topp fem ting å gjøre i Oslo": 511 sessions, 75% engagement rate
- "Topp 5 aktiviteter for barn i Stavanger": 249 sessions, 80% engagement rate

**Why it works:**
- Answers high-frequency search behavior ("what to do in [city]")
- Target audience (families with children) has high purchase potential for car rental
- Format (numbered lists) matches both search intent and modern content consumption
- Oslo is largest market – geographic relevance gives natural traffic

**Format elements:**
- Numbered lists (5-10 items)
- Family focus performs best
- City-specific (Oslo >> other cities)
- Short intro per activity
- Practical info (parking, season, access)

### 2. Owner-Directed "How-to" Articles

**Top performers:**
- "Disse bilene bør du leie ut": 296 sessions, 90% engagement, 84 sec average
- "Bli profesjonell utleier": 340 sessions, 90% engagement, 55 sec
- "Topp 10 vanlige spørsmål fra utleiere": 184 sessions, 80% engagement, 86 sec (!)

**Why it works:**
- Hits audience in "decision stage" – actively considering becoming owner
- Solves concrete problems (which car? how to optimize?)
- Long engagement time indicates high intent and quality
- Evergreen content – not season-bound

**Content elements:**
- Concrete data (income examples, ROI calculations)
- Step-by-step processes
- FAQ format works well
- Professional, data-driven tone
- Long engagement time (54-85 sec) is acceptable for complex topics

### 3. Specific Use-Case Articles

**Top performers:**
- "Lei en bil til bryllupet ditt": 169 sessions, 85% engagement
- "Lei kjøreskolebil til prøvekjøring": 162 sessions, 81% engagement, 6.58% CTR
- "Leie cybertruck": 353 sessions, 90% engagement, 24.62% CTR

**Why it works:**
- High commercial intent – specific need = high conversion potential
- Low competition – niche queries
- Matches exact search intent

## What Performs Poorly

### 1. Case Studies / Customer Stories

**Recurring problem:**
- "Emelie og Lasse bygde campervan": 181 sessions, 49% engagement
- "Aaron og Lena leier ut bil": 132 sessions, 45% engagement
- "Møt Sandra...": 32 sessions, 47% engagement

**Why it fails:**
- Wrong search intent: Users search for "how to rent out car", not "read about Sandra"
- Missing credibility markers: No data, numbers, or concrete takeaways
- Weak structure: Narrative form without scannable elements (lists, bold, callouts)
- Wrong funnel placement – should be social media content, not SEO-focused

**How to fix:**
- Add concrete data (monthly income, utilization rate, ROI)
- Structure with H2/H3 subheadings
- Include actionable takeaways
- Lead with benefits, then tell story

### 2. Content-Product Mismatch

**Example: "Skifte til vinterdekk"**
- 255 sessions, but only 47% engagement, 38 sec average
- 4,570 impressions, 5 clicks (0.11% CTR)

**Problem:**
- Topic is seasonal (October-November)
- Low match with Getaround's core product (car-sharing, not car maintenance)
- Users bounce because content doesn't meet their need (they want to switch tires on their OWN car)

**This is "vanity traffic"** - high impressions, but ZERO business value.

**Fix:**
- Rewrite to "Rent car with winter tires – what you need to know" (product relevance)
- OR deindex/redirect to more relevant content

### 3. Geographic Mismatch

Bergen, Stavanger, Trondheim: Good engagement numbers, but very low traffic compared to Oslo.

**Example:**
- "Topp 5 aktiviteter for barn i Trondheim": 132 sessions, 89% engagement (!)
- "Topp 5 vandreturer i Stavanger": 226 sessions, 61% engagement

**These perform well by definition, but volume is too low to move the needle.**

**Strategy:**
- Either: Prioritize Oslo content (where the volume is)
- Or: Create strategic "city collections" to capture "things to do in Norway" as breadth strategy

## Format Preferences by Content Type

### For Activities/Travel (Renter-focused):

**Best format:**
- Listicle (Topp 5-10)
- Short intro (50-100 words)
- Numbered items with H3 subheadings
- 100-150 words per activity
- Practical info: parking, season, family-friendly, price range
- Natural Getaround integration (not pushy)
- FAQ at end (3-5 questions)

**Target engagement:** 65-80% engagement rate, 25-40 sec engagement time

### For Owner Guides (Owner-focused):

**Best format:**
- How-to structure with clear H2/H3
- Data-driven (income examples, ROI calculations)
- Longer is OK (54-85 sec engagement time is good for complex topics)
- FAQ format works extremely well
- Professional tone, concrete advice

**Target engagement:** 75-90% engagement rate, 45-85 sec engagement time

### For Use-Case Articles (Transactional):

**Best format:**
- Problem-solution structure
- Specific scenario (wedding, driver's test, moving)
- Practical advice
- Clear CTA
- Shorter (15-25 sec engagement time is OK if they convert)

**Target engagement:** 80-90% engagement rate, conversion focus

## Business Fit Validation Checklist

Before writing or optimizing any article, validate:

**✅ Search intent alignment:**
- Do people search this BECAUSE they need to rent a car?
- Or are they searching for general information unrelated to car rental?

**✅ Conversion potential:**
- Can this article drive bookings (renter side)?
- Can this article drive owner registrations (owner side)?

**✅ Product-content fit:**
- Does content naturally link to Getaround products (Connect, long-term, delivery)?
- Or is it generic content applicable to any car?

**🚫 AVOID "vanity traffic":**
- High impressions/sessions but low business value
- Examples: car maintenance, general driving tips, unrelated "how-to"
- These inflate metrics without driving revenue

**Focus on "qualified traffic":**
- Activities in [city] → high intent (planning trip, needs car)
- How to rent out car → high intent (considering becoming owner)
- [Specific car type] rental → high intent (ready to book)
