# Getaround Master Context

Essential information about Getaround Norway for content creation.

## What Getaround Is

Getaround is a peer-to-peer car-sharing marketplace. Private owners and small professional actors list their cars, and renters book via app or web. Selection ranges from small cars and vans to EVs and RVs.

## Products & Delivery Methods

- **Getaround Connect (hardware)**: Unlock and start car with phone. Requires installed box. No physical handover.
- **Native Connect (Tesla)**: Tesla only. Owner connects car directly via Tesla API, no box needed. Functionally equivalent to Connect.
- **Key handover**: Owner and renter meet for key exchange.

**Important**: Each car has ONE method enabled - either Connect (including Native Connect for Tesla) OR key handover. No switching per car or per rental.

- **Long-term rental**: Up to 90 days. Same flow as regular rentals, just longer duration.
- **Getaround for Business (B2B)**: For SMBs. Use from projects to ongoing needs. Vans constitute a large share of trips.

## Competitors & Threats

- **Norway**: Hyre, Bilkollektivet, traditional rental companies
  - Renter side: Hyre is the clearest threat. Traditional players can pressure price.
  - Owner side: Practically a monopoly. No other scaled platforms for listing own car, except niches (RV sites) and FINN.no at smaller scale.
- **Organic search**: Hyre and traditional players often rank strongly.

## Positioning & USPs

**For renters:**
- Breadth of selection
- Nationwide availability (cities, towns, airports, train stations)
- Delivery option where owner offers it

**For owners:**
- Income potential from one car to small fleet
- Scalable operations with simple automations

## Supply, Demand & Geography

- **Strong markets**: Oslo, Tromsø, Bergen
- **Coverage**: Larger cities and airports, including Gardermoen and Flesland
- **Season**: Summer and Christmas are strong. In the north, northern lights season (February-March) is particularly important.
- **Growth focus**: Oslo and Tromsø

## Popular Segments & Use Cases

- **Segments**: Tesla, vans, SUV/winter, city cars, RVs
- **Needs**: Short errands, visits, viewings, pickup/delivery, festivals, skiing, holidays and longer trips
- **Travelers**: International visitors are important, especially in the north. Airport delivery is attractive with lots of luggage or children.

## Challenges & Improvement Areas

**Oslo**: Hyre is aggressive on price and has newer car fleet.

**Cleaning**: Variable level because cars are privately owned. Hyre has fixed washing programs.

**Owner-directed**: Professional, clear and concrete.

**Renter-directed**: Friendly, inspiring and practical.

Avoid exaggerations. Explain internal terms briefly.

**Critical differentiation:**

## Key Terms

- **Getaround Connect (hardware)**: App-opening without meeting. Requires installed box.
- **Native Connect (Tesla)**: Tesla connected via Tesla API. No box.
- **Key handover**: Meeting for key exchange.
- **Long-term rental**: Rental up to 90 days.
- **Instant booking**: Immediate booking, normally on Connect cars.
- **Delivery**: Owner can deliver car to address or airport.
